Mia's wall poster

Jona, Selin and Mia are 11 and in class 6b. Their class is helping the animal shelter: it wants a machine that sorts pictures of dogs and cats. For this it needs artificial intelligence, or AI for short. First, Mia wants to know where AI is already part of her everyday life. She collects things for a wall poster.

On this page you learn to spot AI in your everyday life.

How to spot AI

AI apps recognise something, such as spoken words or animals in photos. Or they suggest something, such as videos you might like. Or they translate into another language. Only people used to be able to do this.

A person programmed the calculator and the alarm clock: they wrote down every rule as an instruction. 3 + 4 makes 7, the alarm rings at 7 o'clock. No AI is needed for that. AI apps, on the other hand, mostly learned what they can do from many examples.

artificial intelligence (AI)

Computer programs that recognise, suggest or translate things. They mostly learned this from many examples, instead of someone programming every rule.
