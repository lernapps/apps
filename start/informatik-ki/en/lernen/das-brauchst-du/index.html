Can you do this already?

In this chapter you train a machine with examples. For this you need two things from the chapter on data. Check yourself quickly:

  • Information and data: I can tell information and data apart and explain that data only become information when someone interprets them.
  • Interpreting data: I can interpret data in their context, for example tell whether the number 21 is an age, a temperature or a time.

An example from the animal shelter

A note at the animal shelter just says “small”. That is data. Only when you know that it is about the size of the dog Waldi does it become information: Waldi is small. (The animals in this app have German names. Krümel, said “KREW-mel”, means “crumb”.)

The animal cards in this chapter carry data like this: ears, snout, size and pupils. You interpret it in context, as with Waldi's card. On the next page you first look for AI in your everyday life.

Exercise

A note at the animal shelter only says the number “4”. What does it mean?

  • You only know once you know what it is about. right answer

    Without context, “4” is just data. It could be an age, a number of things or a room number.

  • A dog is 4 years old.

    That could be. But the note does not say that it is about an age.

  • There are 4 cats at the shelter.

    That could be too. But without context you cannot know.

Data only becomes information when you know its context.
