Glossary

The terms of this app, from A to Z, each with the page that explains it.

artificial intelligence (AI)

Computer programs that recognise, suggest or translate things. They mostly learned this from many examples, instead of someone programming every rule.

Explained on the page AI in everyday life

bias

The training data shows the world in a one-sided, skewed way. The system takes on a pattern that only holds in the data.

Explained on the page Skewed training data

decision tree

A rule made of questions. Each answer leads to the next question or to a result. Selin's rule is a decision tree with one question and two branches.

Explained on the page Finding a rule from examples

feature

Something you can observe about an example, such as the shape of the ears.

Explained on the page Finding a rule from examples

label

The right answer for an example; for us “dog” or “cat”.

Explained on the page Finding a rule from examples

machine learning

A computer learns from example data, instead of someone programming every rule.

Explained on the page Training the machine

nearest neighbour

The example in the bag that is most similar to a new animal: the closest one, like the person next to you in class. The machine's method is named after it.

Explained on the page Training the machine

training data

The examples with labels that something learns from: for us, the cards in the training bag.

Explained on the page Finding a rule from examples
