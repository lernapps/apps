Der Text ist ein eigener Text nach Fakten aus Wikipedia („Mod (subculture)“, „Punk subculture“, „Grime music“), Stand 24. November 2026. Die Zahlen sind die Absätze.

1 Young people in Britain have often used clothes and music to show who they are. A group with its own style, music and ideas is called a subculture. This text looks at three of them: the mods, the punks and the fans of grime.

2 The mods began in London in the late 1950s. The word comes from modernist, because the first mods listened to modern jazz. Young men often wore tailor-made suits and skinny ties, and many rode scooters. Over their clothes they put on parkas to keep them clean. Many young women had short haircuts and wore trousers or shirts, flat shoes and little make-up.

3 The mods liked jazz best, but they also listened to soul, rhythm and blues and ska. They met in coffee bars, which stayed open until the early hours, while pubs closed at about 11 pm. Some coffee bars had jukeboxes, and in the mid-1960s the mods were fans of pop groups with rhythm and blues influences.

4 In the early and mid-1960s, some mods had well-publicised fights with a rival group, the rockers. By 1965 the conflict was getting smaller, and mod style became a part of the general fashion of "Swinging London". By the early 1970s the subculture had declined. There was a mod revival in Britain in the late 1970s, and mods still exist today on a smaller scale.

5 Punk came later. According to Wikipedia, the subculture appeared in New York in 1974 and in the United Kingdom in 1976. Punk rock is loud, and most songs are short and simple, with only a few chords. A typical band has a singer, one or two guitarists, a bass player and a drummer.

6 Punks wore T-shirts, leather jackets and boots. Some had brightly coloured hair or a spiked mohawk, and tattoos and jewellery were part of the look. Many early punks came from working-class families. Their main ideas were that people should be free and should not simply follow the rules. Many liked to do things themselves: some made their own magazines, called zines, and some published their own music.

7 Grime is a much younger sound. It began in East London in the early 2000s and grew out of UK garage, with influences from jungle, dancehall and hip-hop. The beat is fast, usually around 140 beats per minute, and the MCs are important. Their lyrics often describe life in the city. At first the music spread on pirate radio stations. In the middle of the 2000s it reached a larger audience in the UK. Writers sometimes compare grime with punk, because both grew out of simple technology and a do-it-yourself attitude.

8 What do the three groups have in common? Each used fashion and music to create a feeling of belonging. Some people say this is a strong reason to join a group. Others prefer to follow no group at all.
