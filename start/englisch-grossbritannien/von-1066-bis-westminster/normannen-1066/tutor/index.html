Die Tafel am Tower

Vokabeln (Englisch – Deutsch): Randglossar

  • Anglo-Saxon – angelsächsisch
  • duke – Herzog
  • conquest – Eroberung
  • rebellion – Aufstand
  • came (come), became (become) – kam, wurde (Past Simple von come und become)
  • founded (found) – gründete
  • Old English – Altenglisch (die Sprache vor 1066)
  • Anglo-Norman French – das Französisch der Normannen in England

The year 1066 · Information board, Tower of London

  1. After King Edward died, Harold Godwinson was the Anglo-Saxon king of England.
  2. On 14 October 1066, William, Duke of Normandy, and his army fought Harold's English army.
  3. The battle was about eleven kilometres north-west of Hastings, near a place called Battle in East Sussex.
  4. The Normans won. This was the beginning of the Norman Conquest.
  5. William the Conqueror was the first Norman king of England. He was king from 1066 and defeated rebellions.
  6. Near the end of 1066, the Normans founded the Tower of London, a castle on the north side of the River Thames.
  7. The White Tower gives the castle its name. William built it around 1078.
  8. After 1066, the English language changed. A new language grew out of Old English, under the influence of Anglo-Norman French and other languages. It is called Middle English.
  9. People spoke Middle English until the late fifteenth century. In different parts of England it was very different.
