Im Museum · ein Gespräch

  1. Sophie: Excuse me, I have some questions about two famous people from English history.
  2. Guide: Of course! Which two?
  3. Sophie: Henry VIII and Elizabeth I. Who was Henry VIII?
  4. Guide: He was king of England from 22 April 1509 until his death in 1547.
  5. Sophie: Why is he so famous?
  6. Guide: People know him for his six marriages. He also broke with the Roman Catholic Church. He founded the Church of England, and so the church in England changed.
  7. Sophie: And who was Elizabeth I?
  8. Guide: She was queen of England and Ireland from 17 November 1558 until 1603.
  9. Sophie: Was she a Tudor?
  10. Guide: Yes, she was from the House of Tudor. She was the last Tudor monarch, and no other Tudor monarch was monarch for longer.
  11. Sophie: Is there a time named after her?
  12. Guide: Yes, the Elizabethan era. It has her name.
  13. Sophie: Thank you very much! That helps me a lot.

Aufgabe

Schreibe das fehlende Wort. Es steht im Gespräch.

Tipp

Die Wörter stehen in Zeile 8 und Zeile 10.

  1. Elizabeth I war die Letzte aus dem Haus Tudor. → Elizabeth I was the … ruler of the House of Tudor.
  2. Elizabeth I war Königin von England und Irland. → Elizabeth I was queen of England and ….
Lösung
  1. Elizabeth I was the last ruler of the House of Tudor.
  2. Elizabeth I was queen of England and Ireland.

Aufgabe

Wähle die richtige Form. So sagt man ein Jahr oder ein Datum.

Tipp

Bei Daten sagt man the und die Ordnungszahl, bei Jahren zwei Zahlen.

  1. Henry VIII wurde 1509 König. → Henry VIII became king in ….
  2. Elizabeth I wurde am 17. November 1558 Königin. → Elizabeth I became queen on the … of November fifteen fifty-eight.
Lösung
  1. Henry VIII became king in fifteen oh nine.
  2. Elizabeth I became queen on the seventeenth of November fifteen fifty-eight.

Offene Aufgabe

Schreibe drei bis vier Sätze auf Englisch: Why were Henry VIII and Elizabeth I important for England? Meinungen sind erlaubt.

Eine gute Antwort enthält:

  • Du nennst eine Veränderung unter Henry VIII, zum Beispiel bei der Kirche. Nachlesen
  • Du sagst, wie lange einer der beiden regierte, mit Jahreszahlen. Nachlesen
  • Du sagst mit I think, was du wichtig findest. (Form)
Lösung

Henry VIII broke with the Roman Catholic Church and founded the Church of England. Elizabeth I was queen from fifteen fifty-eight until sixteen oh three. I think they were important because the church and the country changed in their time.
