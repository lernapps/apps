Chemical reactions

You recognise chemical reactions, tell them from changes of state, explain conservation of mass and write simple reaction equations.

  1. How to recognise a reaction Colour, gas, heat or light can hint at a chemical reaction. Only a new substance is certain.
  2. Reaction or change of state? In a change of state the substance stays the same; in a chemical reaction a new one is formed.
  3. Mass stays the same In a reaction the total mass stays the same. A balance experiment shows it, and atoms explain it.
  4. Reaction equations A reaction equation shows which substances react. There must be the same number of atoms on both sides.
