Why

When wood burns, no wood is left. When effervescent powder fizzes in water, something has happened that you cannot undo. In chemistry this is called a chemical reaction. You cannot see inside the substance, but you can recognise a reaction from what you observe.

Rule

Chemical reaction

In a chemical reaction substances turn into other substances. The new substances have different properties from the old ones.

Hints of a reaction are for example a change of colour, a rising gas, heat or light. They are only hints: a radiator also warms a substance, and boiling water bubbles without reacting.

Only one thing is certain: a new substance is formed whose properties differ from before. So always compare the properties of the substances before and after, for example colour, smell or whether they burn.

Example

Magnesium burns

A silvery magnesium ribbon burns with a bright white light. Afterwards a white powder is left. You observe light, heat and a substance that looks and behaves differently from the metal before. The new white powder proves the reaction.

Practice

Exercise

Which observations can hint at a chemical reaction?

  • A solid turns black when heated. right answer

    A new colour can mean a new substance. It is a hint, but not proof yet.

  • Gas bubbles rise when two liquids are mixed. right answer

    A new gas can be formed in a reaction. A hint at a new substance.

  • A piece of metal glows brightly and gives off heat. right answer

    Light and heat often show a reaction, for example when burning.

  • A piece of ice lies on the table and gets smaller.

    The ice melts. It stays water; no new substance is formed.

Colour, gas, light and heat are hints. When ice melts, it stays the same substance.

Exercise

When bubbles rise while water is heated, that proves a chemical reaction.

Right answer: The statement is false.

Boiling forms water vapour. That is still water, just as a gas. A rising gas is only a hint, not proof.

Exercise

How can you be sure that a chemical reaction has taken place?

  • A new substance is formed. right answer

    That is the certain sign: the substances have turned into other substances.

  • The substance gets warmer.

    A substance can also get warmer without reacting, for example on a radiator.

  • The substance changes its state of matter.

    That is a change of state. The substance stays the same.

Only a new substance shows a reaction for sure. Heat or a change of state is not enough.
