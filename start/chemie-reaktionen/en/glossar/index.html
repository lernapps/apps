Glossary

The terms of this app, from A to Z, each with the page that explains it.

Boiling point

The temperature at which a liquid becomes gaseous. For a pure substance it is always the same.

Explained on the page States of matter – a short recap

Change of state

In a change of state such as melting or evaporating the substance stays the same. Only the state of matter changes.

Explained on the page Reaction or change of state?

Chemical reaction

In a chemical reaction substances turn into other substances. The new substances have different properties from the old ones.

Explained on the page How to recognise a reaction

Coefficient

A coefficient is the big number in front of a formula. It tells how often the formula occurs.

Explained on the page Reaction equations

Compound

A compound consists of atoms of different elements joined in a fixed ratio, for example water or carbon dioxide.

Explained on the page Symbols and formulas – a short recap

Conservation of mass

In a chemical reaction the total mass of the substances afterwards is just as large as before.

Explained on the page Mass stays the same

Formula

A formula uses element symbols to show which atoms a substance is made of. A small subscript number, the index, tells how many atoms there are.

Explained on the page Symbols and formulas – a short recap

Melting point

The temperature at which a solid becomes liquid. For a pure substance it is always the same.

Explained on the page States of matter – a short recap

Molecule

A molecule is a small particle made of several joined atoms, for example a water molecule.

Explained on the page Symbols and formulas – a short recap

Particle model

In the particle model every substance consists of very small particles, which you imagine as balls.

Explained on the page Particle model – a short recap

Property of a substance

A property of a substance is something you can use to recognise a substance and to tell it from other substances, for example its colour, its smell, its hardness or its density.

Explained on the page Properties of substances – a short recap

Reaction equation

A reaction equation describes a reaction with formulas. The starting substances are on the left of the arrow, the products on the right.

Explained on the page Reaction equations
