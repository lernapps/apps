[1] The Eiffel Tower was built for the World's Fair of 1889 in Paris, which celebrated one hundred years since the French Revolution. The first sketch was ready in May 1884, almost three years before the building work began on 28 January 1887.

[2] Before the first part came to the site, the drawing office had produced 1,700 general drawings and 3,629 detailed drawings. The work had to be exact: the position of every rivet hole was fixed to within one millimetre. The parts were made in a factory near Paris and arrived on horse-drawn carts. Nothing was drilled on the site: if a part did not fit, it went back to the factory. In the end, 18,038 pieces were joined with 2.5 million rivets.

[3] Many people did not believe in the project. Before the work began, nobody had built a structure higher than 200 metres, and a group of artists sent a petition against the tower. Nevertheless, the workers carried on and did not stop the building work. The tower was finished on 31 March 1889. By then, they had been building for more than two years.

[4] The World's Fair opened on 6 May 1889, but the tower was not open to visitors until 15 May. The lifts were not ready yet: the engineers were still working on them when the first guests arrived. Nearly 30,000 people climbed the 1,710 steps to the top before the lifts started on 26 May.

[5] Tickets cost two francs for the first level, three for the second and five for the top. By the end of the fair, 1,896,987 people had visited the tower. Gustave Eiffel was allowed to keep the income from the tower for the next twenty years.

[6] The Eiffel Tower was the tallest structure in the world from 1889 until 1930, when the Chrysler Building became taller.

Die Welle bis zu einem Punkt

Beim present perfect progressive reicht die Welle bis now. Das past perfect progressive rückt sie in die Vergangenheit. Die Welle beginnt früher und reicht nur bis zu einem Punkt der Vergangenheit. Der Bogen darüber zeigt, wie lange sie schon lief.

Klassenzimmer-Poster · Das past perfect progressive
PersonFormBeispiel
alle Personenhad been workingShe had been waiting for an hour when the bus came.
Verneinunghadn't been workingThey hadn't been sleeping well.
FrageHad … been working?Had she been working for long?

Merke

had been + Verb mit -ing, für alle Personen gleich. Die Dauer steht oft mit for (for an hour) oder since (since Monday). Der Zeitpunkt der Vergangenheit steht im simple past (when the bus came). Das past perfect ohne -ing zeigt dagegen, dass etwas fertig war (She had finished her homework). Das past perfect progressive zeigt, wie lange etwas lief (She had been doing her homework for an hour). Zustandsverben (Verben, die etwas beschreiben, das man nicht aktiv tut) wie know bilden keine Verlaufsform: I had known him for years, nicht had been knowing.

  • Mehr als zwei Jahre richtige Antwort

  • Fast drei Jahre

    Fast drei Jahre lagen zwischen der ersten Skizze und dem Baubeginn, nicht zwischen Baubeginn und Fertigstellung.

Aufgabe

Wähle die richtige Form.

  1. Die Kinder spielten schon seit Stunden draußen, als es zu regnen begann. → The children … outside for hours when it began to rain.
  2. Mein Vater war die ganze Nacht gefahren, als er die Küste erreichte. → My father … all night when he reached the coast.
  3. Die Band übte schon drei Wochen, als das Konzert stattfand. → The band … for three weeks when the concert took place.
  4. Wir warteten schon seit einer Stunde, als der Zug endlich kam. → We … for an hour when the train finally arrived.
Lösung
  1. The children had been playing outside for hours when it began to rain.
  2. My father had been driving all night when he reached the coast.
  3. The band had been practising for three weeks when the concert took place.
  4. We had been waiting for an hour when the train finally arrived.

Aufgabe

Schreibe den ganzen Satz im past perfect progressive.

Tipp

Frag dich, was schon lief, bevor etwas anderes passierte. Das bekommt had been und das Verb mit -ing.

  1. she · learn · Spanish · for two years · before she moved to Madrid
  2. the team · train · all week · when the injury happened
  3. I · work · in the garden · since eight o'clock · when it started to rain
  4. they · not · sleep · well · before the storm
Lösung
  1. She had been learning Spanish for two years before she moved to Madrid. (auch richtig: She'd been learning Spanish for two years before she moved to Madrid.)
  2. The team had been training all week when the injury happened.
  3. I had been working in the garden since eight o'clock when it started to rain. (auch richtig: I'd been working in the garden since eight o'clock when it started to rain.)
  4. They hadn't been sleeping well before the storm. (auch richtig: They had not been sleeping well before the storm.)

Offene Aufgabe

Schreibe drei bis vier Sätze über ein Ereignis, bei dem etwas schon lange lief, als etwas anderes passierte, zum Beispiel ein Spiel, ein Konzert oder eine Reise. Nenne, wie lange es schon dauerte.

Eine gute Antwort enthält:

  • Mindestens ein Satz im past perfect progressive (had been und das Verb mit -ing). Nachlesen
  • Die Dauer ist genannt, zum Beispiel mit for oder since. Nachlesen
  • Der spätere Zeitpunkt steht im simple past. Nachlesen
  • Ganze Sätze mit Verb und Punkt am Ende. (Form)
Lösung

The crowd had been waiting for three hours when the band finally came on stage. Some fans had been standing in the rain since the morning. When the first song started, everybody cheered.

Wie es weitergeht

Am Zeitstrahl hängt jetzt eine Welle, die in der Vergangenheit bis zu einem Punkt reicht. Auf der nächsten Seite geht es um etwas, das früher oft passierte und heute nicht mehr gilt, eine graue Punktreihe, die heute endet.
