Four layers of protection

What protects against what (as the BSI describes it)
ProtectionWhat it does
firewallchecks the data that goes into and out of the device
antivirusrecognises known malware; must always be up to date
updatefixes mistakes and closes security holes in programs
screen lockkeeps out strangers who have the device in their hands
security hole

A mistake in a program through which malware or attackers can get in. An update closes it.

Remember

No protection catches everything. Antivirus mainly recognises known malware, often not brand-new malware. That is why all four belong together, plus your care and a backup.

Settings on the tablet

Mia's new tablet

  1. Turn on the screen lock with a PIN.
  2. Turn on automatic updates.
  3. Only get apps from the device's app store.
  4. For each app, check which permissions it wants. Does a torch app need your contacts? No, so do not install it.

This is what the BSI advises for smartphones and tablets. More from the BSI (in German)
