How many passwords are there?

A guessing program tries all passwords. How long that takes depends on how many there are.

All the characters a password may be made of, for example 26 lowercase letters or 10 digits.

A simulation: you choose the length (4 to 24 characters) and the kinds of characters, and it shows how long a computer needs on average to guess, if it tries 10 billion passwords per second. What this app assumes

The computer tries 10 billion passwords per second, and on average it finds the right one after half of them. We count special characters as 33. Real attackers are often faster: they try lists of names, words and years of birth first, because people choose passwords like that. “selin2014” is near the top of such a list.

Protecting your account

The BSI, Germany's office for information security, advises: “The longer, the better.” As a minimum it names 8 characters from four kinds; the simulation shows why that is only the minimum. A very long one is better (the BSI says 20 to 25 characters). And: use each password for one account only. More from the BSI (in German)

password manager

A program that keeps your passwords encrypted, that is in a secret code that can only be read with your main password. You only need to remember one long main password.

two-factor authentication

You log in with two things of different kinds, for example something you know (a password) and something you have (a phone with a code). If the password gets out, the account usually stays protected, as long as nobody also has the code.
