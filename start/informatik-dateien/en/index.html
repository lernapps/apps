My files: organise, store, protect

Mia is 11 and in class 6b. She has a tablet. On it there are photos, songs and texts for school. You help Mia tidy it up: you put files into folders, find them again by their path and compare the tablet, a USB stick and the cloud. For ages 10 to 12. How to protect your files will come later in a chapter of its own.

Contents

  1. Folders, paths and storage

    You tidy up Mia's tablet, find files again by their path and compare where files can be stored.

    1. What you need here (recap) A short reminder: storage, cut and paste, the tree, bits and bytes.
    2. Files and folders Tidying Mia's tablet: putting files into folders and finding them again. The folder tree and a file manager.
    3. File paths A path is the way from the root to the file: reading paths, writing them and using them in a file manager.
    4. Comparing storage locations Tablet, USB stick and cloud: how much space, when you can get to your files and what can happen.
  2. Backing up and protecting data

    You back up Mia's files, make secure passwords, spot how malware gets onto a device and protect it.

    1. What you need here (recap) A short reminder: computing systems in everyday life, hardware and software.
    2. Backing up data Backup copies in a second place and saved versions: how Mia's files stay safe even when the tablet is gone.
    3. Secure passwords Why a long password is hard to guess: a simulation, the BSI's advice and how to protect your accounts.
    4. Malware Viruses, worms, Trojans and ransomware: how they get onto a device and what you do then.
    5. Security software and updates Firewall, antivirus, updates and settings on the tablet: what they protect. With the rule of the chapter.
