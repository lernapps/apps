Glossary

The terms of this app, from A to Z, each with the page that explains it.

cycle

A cycle leads along edges back to the node it started from. It uses no edge and no other node twice.

Explained on the page From tree to graph

dead end

A dead end is a page without a link. Anyone there cannot go on with any click.

Explained on the page Class 7b's town guide

directed graph

In a directed graph, every edge has a direction. You draw it as an arrow. A path may only use arrows in their direction.

Explained on the page Edges with a direction

graph

A graph is made of nodes and edges. An edge connects two nodes. Any node may be connected to any other node.

Explained on the page From tree to graph

home page

The home page is the page where you start reading a hypertext. From it you can reach the other pages through links.

Explained on the page A graph made of pages

hypertext

A hypertext is made of pages that are joined by links. As a graph, the pages are nodes and the links are arrows.

Explained on the page A graph made of pages

link

A link is a place on a page that leads to another page. It is only on one of the two pages.

Explained on the page Edges with a direction

link text

The link text is the word you click on. It tells you where the link leads.

Explained on the page A graph made of pages

path

A path is a sequence of edges that join up. It starts at one node, ends at a node and visits no node twice. The length of a path is the number of its edges.

Explained on the page From tree to graph
