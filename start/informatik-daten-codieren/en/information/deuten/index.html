What is 21?

Mia is packing for the school trip. She sees the number 21 everywhere. But each time, the 21 means something different.

On the page before you saw that data only becomes information with a context. On this page you will work out what data means yourself.

Exercise

Where Mia sees 21, and what it means. Match them.

Choose the match for each entry.

Right answer: 21 on the thermometer – It is 21 degrees.; 21:00 on the alarm clock – It is 9 o'clock in the evening.; 21 candles on her cousin's cake – Her cousin is turning 21.; 21 on the sports shirt – That is the shirt number.

The same data, the number 21, means something different depending on the context. The thermometer, the clock or the cake tells you how to read the 21.

Solution

Right answer: 21 on the thermometer – It is 21 degrees.; 21:00 on the alarm clock – It is 9 o'clock in the evening.; 21 candles on her cousin's cake – Her cousin is turning 21.; 21 on the sports shirt – That is the shirt number.

The context is often written next to it

In a table, the context is often in the heading of a column, or in a unit such as o'clock, degrees or euros. Ms Berg has sent the plan for the first day at Seeberg. The times use the 24-hour clock, as in Germany: 14 means 2 pm.

Plan for Monday at Seeberg
WhatTimeHow many children
Arrival1126
Walk around the lake14 to 1718
Boat trip158
Night walk2121

Remember

Always read the column heading first, then the number. In the row “Night walk” there are two 21s. One 21 is a time, the other is a number of children.
