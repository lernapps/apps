Context: the page “Information and data”

Telling information and data apart: a note with data only becomes information when you know the context.

A note on the floor

Jona, Selin and Mia are 11 years old and in class 6b. Jona finds a note on the classroom floor. There are only a few symbols on it. Jona turns the note this way and that. What is it supposed to mean?

On this page you will learn what data is and when it becomes information.

Picture: Jona, with short ginger hair and a green hoodie, and Selin, with a dark ponytail and an orange jacket, are standing in the classroom. Between them a yellow note lies on the floor. Caption: Jona and Selin in class 6b’s classroom

Picture: A yellow note. It says: 6b · 11.5. · 8:00. Below that, a train is drawn. Caption: The note that Jona found

  • Exercise zettel-klar (below)

Data needs a context

Selin sees the note and laughs: “That's from Ms Berg, our class teacher! On Monday, 11 May, we are going to the youth hostel at Seeberg. We meet at 8 o'clock at the station, where the train leaves.” Selin knows the context: Ms Berg is planning class 6b's school trip. In Germany, 11.5. means 11 May, and Seeberg is a place by a lake (See is German for lake). That is why she understands the note.

  • data: Data is symbols written down by fixed rules, for example numbers, words or a date. That way you can store it and pass it on.

  • information: Information is the meaning that someone gets from data because they know the context.

Remember: Remember

Ms Berg had some information in her head. She wrote it down as data. Selin reads the data and works out what it means. That way the data becomes information again. Jona did not have the context: for him it was only data.

Exercise zettel-klar

  • Task: If you only have the note, you know for sure what it is about.
  • Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
  • Solution: The statement is false.
  • Explanation: Without context, “8:00” could be a time or a score, and “6b” could be a class or a seat in the cinema.

Exercise weg-der-nachricht

  • Task: Put the path of the message in the right order.
  • Answer format: put the items in the right order (the app shows them shuffled)
  • Solution: 1. Ms Berg knows when and where class 6b will meet., 2. Ms Berg writes the data on a note., 3. Selin reads the note., 4. Selin understands that class 6b meets on Monday at 8 o'clock at the station.
  • Explanation: First the information is in Ms Berg's head, then it becomes data on the note. When Selin reads it and works out what it means, it becomes information again.
  • Link: ../?via=tutor#weg-der-nachricht

Exercise daten-zaehlen

  • Task: How many pieces of data are on the note? The train counts, the dots in between do not.
  • Answer format: type a number
  • Solution: 4
  • Explanation: There are 4 pieces of data on the note: the class, the date, the time and the train for the place.
  • Typical mistakes and what the app says:
    • 3: Look again: 6b, 11.5., 8:00 and the train. The class is a piece of data too.
    • 1: There is one note, but it has several pieces of data on it. Count each piece separately.
  • Link: ../?via=tutor#daten-zaehlen

Exercise information-erkennen

  • Task: Which sentence is the information that Selin gets from the note?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Class 6b meets on 11 May at 8 o'clock at the station. – This is how Selin understands the data, because she knows that Ms Berg is planning the school trip.
    • ✗ 6b · 11.5. · 8:00 and a train – That is the data, exactly as it is on the note. The information is what it means.
    • ✗ A train leaves at 8 o'clock. – The note does not say that. Class 6b only meets at the station.
  • Solution: Class 6b meets on 11 May at 8 o'clock at the station.
  • Explanation: The information is what the data means in the context of the school trip.
  • Link: ../?via=tutor#information-erkennen

Exercise daten-oder-information

  • Task: Match each sentence to what it describes, data or information.
  • Answer format: match each item on the left with one on the right
  • Solution: The note says “8:00”. – data, without a meaning yet; Selin knows that she has to be at the station at 8 o'clock. – information, understood from the data
  • Explanation: Symbols on the note are data. What Selin understands from them is information.
