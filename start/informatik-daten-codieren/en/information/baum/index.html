Root at the top, leaves at the bottom

tree

A tree shows data that fits inside other data. It is made of nodes and edges. At the very top there is one node, the root. Nodes with no nodes below them are called leaves.

Remember

A tree in computer science is upside down: the root is at the top, the leaves are at the bottom. Every node except the root hangs from exactly one node above it. That is why there is exactly one path from the root to each leaf.

Example

The tournament tree

In the evening, four children play table tennis. Whoever wins moves up one row. At the bottom are the four children, they are the leaves. The root at the top is the child who won the tournament.
