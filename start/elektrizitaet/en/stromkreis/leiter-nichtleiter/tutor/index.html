Context: the page “Conductors and insulators”

Metals conduct electricity; rubber, glass and plastic do not. That is how you can tell what cables are made of.

The idea

Inside, a cable is made of copper; outside, of plastic. Why not all copper or all plastic? Because some materials let current through and others do not.

What to remember

  • Conductor: A conductor lets electric current through well. All metals are conductors, for example copper, iron and aluminium.

  • Insulator: An insulator (also called a non-conductor) hardly lets any electric current through, for example plastic, rubber, glass, wood and porcelain.

Important materials and whether they conduct electricity:

  • Material | Conductor or insulator?
  • Copper | Conductor
  • Iron | Conductor
  • Aluminium | Conductor
  • Graphite (pencil lead) | Conductor
  • Plastic | Insulator
  • Rubber | Insulator
  • Glass | Insulator
  • Wood (dry) | Insulator

Remember: Water can conduct, too

Tap water and salt water conduct electricity because substances are dissolved in them. That is why wet hands and electricity are a dangerous mix. More on the page “Staying safe”.

Example

Example: Why a cable has two parts

The copper wire inside is a conductor: the current flows in it. The sheath of plastic is an insulator: it protects you when you touch the cable and stops two wires from touching each other.

Exercise ln-leiter

  • Task: Which materials are conductors?
  • Answer format: choose all right answers
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Copper – Copper is a metal and conducts electricity very well. That is why cables are made of copper inside.
    • ✓ Iron – Iron is a metal too and conducts electricity.
    • ✗ Rubber – Rubber does not conduct electricity. That is why it is suitable as a sheath around cables and on handles.
    • ✗ Glass – Glass does not conduct electricity and is an insulator.
  • Solution: Copper; Iron
  • Explanation: All metals are conductors. Rubber and glass are insulators.

Exercise ln-huelle

  • Task: What is the sheath of a cable, which protects you, made of?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Plastic, an insulator – Plastic does not let current through. That is why you can touch the sheath safely.
    • ✗ Copper, a conductor – Copper is the wire inside. It conducts the current and would be dangerous as a sheath.
    • ✗ Iron, a conductor – Iron conducts electricity and is not suitable as a protective sheath.
  • Solution: Plastic, an insulator
  • Explanation: The sheath must be an insulator so that the current flows only in the wire and you are protected.
