Context: the page “Effects of electric current”

Current can produce heat, light and magnetism. Many devices work this way.

The idea

You cannot see current. But you can see what it does: it makes lamps shine, toasters hot and bells ring. These things are called effects of current.

What to remember

Effects of electric current, with examples:

  • Effect | What happens | Example
  • Heating effect | The wire gets warm or hot | Toaster, hairdryer, hob, incandescent bulb
  • Light effect | The current makes something glow | LED lamp, fluorescent tube
  • Magnetic effect | The current creates a magnetic field | Bell, electromagnet on a crane

You can use each of these effects. Sometimes an effect occurs that you do not want: a thin cable with too much current flowing through it gets hot. That can start a fire.

Example

Example: The electric bell

When you press the bell button, current flows through a coil. It becomes an electromagnet, pulls a small hammer towards it and strikes the bell. That is the magnetic effect of current.

Exercise wk-kabel

  • Task: If too much current flows through a thin cable, it can get hot.
  • Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
  • Solution: The statement is true.
  • Explanation: That is the heating effect of current. Too much current makes a thin cable hot and can cause a fire.
  • Link: ../?von=tutor#wk-kabel

Exercise wk-gluehlampe

  • Task: Which effect of current does an incandescent bulb use to shine?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ The thin wire in the bulb gets so hot that it glows. – The wire heats up because of the current and gives off light. The heating effect creates the light here.
    • ✗ A magnet attracts a part inside the bulb. – The magnetic effect is used by a bell, for example, not by an incandescent bulb.
    • ✗ The glass of the bulb is charged by the current. – The glass is an insulator and does not shine by itself.
  • Solution: The thin wire in the bulb gets so hot that it glows.
  • Explanation: In an incandescent bulb a thin wire is heated by the current until it glows and gives off light.
  • Link: ../?von=tutor#wk-gluehlampe
