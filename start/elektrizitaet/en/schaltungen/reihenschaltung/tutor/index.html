Context: the page “Series circuit”

In a series circuit the lamps are connected one after the other. If one fails, all go out.

The idea

You can connect two lamps to a battery in two ways. The first way is called a series circuit: the lamps hang one behind the other like beads on a string.

What to remember

  • Series circuit: In a series circuit the parts are connected one behind the other. There is only one path for the current.

Picture: A circuit with a battery and two lamps that sit next to each other at the top, in the same wire. The current has only one path. Caption: The current has to pass through both lamps, one after the other. There is no other path. Labels: 1 battery, 2 lamp 1, 3 lamp 2.

It follows that:

  • If one lamp is broken or unscrewed, the circuit is broken and all lamps go out.
  • A switch in the series switches all lamps on and off together.
  • The more lamps you connect in series, the dimmer they shine.

Example

Example: The old string of lights

In some older strings of lights all the little bulbs are connected in series. If you unscrew one bulb, the whole string goes out. Then you look for the broken bulb until the string lights up again.

Exercise rs-ausfall

  • Task: In a series circuit of two lamps, the second keeps shining when the first is unscrewed.
  • Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
  • Solution: The statement is false.
  • Explanation: In a series circuit there is only one path for the current. If a lamp is missing, the circuit is open and the other goes out too.
  • Link: ../?von=tutor#rs-ausfall

Exercise rs-wege

  • Task: How many paths does the current have in a series circuit?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Exactly one. – The parts hang one behind the other, so the current has only one way around the circuit.
    • ✗ One for each lamp. – Separate paths for each lamp exist in a parallel circuit, not in a series circuit.
    • ✗ None; the lamps shine because of the battery. – Without a path for the current no lamp shines. The circuit must be closed.
  • Solution: Exactly one.
  • Explanation: In a series circuit there is only one path, on which the current flows through all parts one after the other.
  • Link: ../?von=tutor#rs-wege

Exercise rs-schalterplatz

  • Task: In a series circuit with a battery, a switch and two lamps it does not matter whether the switch sits before, between or after the lamps.
  • Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
  • Solution: The statement is true.
  • Explanation: The current runs through all parts one after the other. It does not matter where the switch sits: when you open it, the circuit is broken and all lamps go out.
  • Link: ../?von=tutor#rs-schalterplatz
