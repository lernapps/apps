Context: the page “Switches and circuit symbols”

A switch opens and closes the circuit. In a circuit diagram you draw parts as circuit symbols.

The idea

If you want to write down a circuit, you do not have to draw cables and lamps. Every part has a short circuit symbol. That way people all over the world read the same diagram.

What to remember

  • Switch: A switch opens or closes the circuit. Closed, the current flows; open, none flows.

  • Circuit diagram: A circuit diagram shows a circuit with circuit symbols instead of drawings of the parts.

Picture: Four circuit symbols one below the other. Battery, two bars, a long thin one and a short thick one. Lamp, a circle with a cross. Open switch, a line with a gap that slants upwards. Closed switch, an unbroken line. Caption: The most important circuit symbols. With the open switch the line is broken; with the closed switch it is unbroken. Labels: 1 battery, 2 lamp, 3 open switch, 4 closed switch.

Circuit symbols and parts:

  • Part | How to recognise the circuit symbol
  • Battery | two bars, a long thin one and a short thick one
  • Lamp | a circle with a cross inside
  • Open switch | a line with a gap; the current does not flow
  • Closed switch | an unbroken line; the current flows

Example

Example: A circuit diagram in words

A circuit diagram shows: battery, then a switch, then a lamp, then back to the battery. If the circuit symbol of the switch is unbroken, the switch is closed and the lamp lights up. If it shows a gap, the switch is open and the lamp stays dark.

Exercise sz-zuordnen

  • Task: Match each circuit symbol with the part.
  • Answer format: match each item on the left with one on the right
  • Solution: a circle with a cross – Lamp; two bars, a long one and a short thick one – Battery; a line with a gap – open switch
  • Explanation: The circle with a cross stands for the lamp, the two bars for the battery, and the gap in the line shows an open switch.

Exercise sz-offen

  • Task: A switch whose circuit symbol has a gap is closed.
  • Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
  • Solution: The statement is false.
  • Explanation: The gap means that the circuit is broken there. The switch is open and no current flows.
  • Link: ../?von=tutor#sz-offen

Exercise sz-schalter

  • Task: A lamp and a switch are in series in a circuit. The lamp is lit. What do you know about the switch?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ It is closed. – Only when the switch is closed is the whole circuit closed, so that the current can flow.
    • ✗ It is open. – With an open switch the circuit is broken and the lamp stays dark.
    • ✗ You cannot tell. – Yes, you can. If the lamp is lit, the circuit must be closed everywhere, including at the switch.
  • Solution: It is closed.
  • Explanation: The lamp only lights up when the current can flow through the switch. So it must be closed.
  • Link: ../?von=tutor#sz-schalter

Exercise schaltung-bauen

  • What to do: You build a circuit from lamps, switches and wires that does what the task asks, switch the power on and test it.
  • Task: „{task}“
  • Answer format: The circuit built on the board (lamp, switch or wire per place: choose a part, then tap the place); then “Check” below the board. Checking switches the power on.
  • What counts as right: The circuit behaves as the task says: lamps shine, switches and unscrewed lamps act as asked. Any build that does this counts, not only the model solution; an additional switch is only a mistake where the task names a fixed number.
  • Typical mistakes (the app's message fits one of them):
    • F1: The wrong number of lamps or switches.
    • F2: One branch stays empty while the other holds a lamp: a short circuit, the lamps stay dark.
    • F3: The parts are right, but a switch switches the wrong lamps or the lamps are in series instead of parallel.
