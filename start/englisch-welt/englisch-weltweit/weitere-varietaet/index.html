Eine Sprache, viele Varietäten

What is a variety?

  1. English is spoken all over the world, and it does not sound or look the same everywhere. A form of English with its own typical sounds, words, grammar and spelling is called a variety.
  2. Many varieties have a standard form that is used in schools, in the news and in books. A variety with its own features is not wrong English.

Australian English

  1. Australian English began to grow apart from British English after 1788. The first settlers came from many parts of Britain and Ireland, and by the 1820s the children born in the colony already sounded different.
  2. Today, English is the common language of Australia, and it is quite similar all over the country. Australian English has its own dictionaries, for example the Macquarie Dictionary.
  3. The spelling is close to British spelling: colour, honour and realise. The r at the end of a syllable is not pronounced.
  4. Friends are often called mates, a barbecue is a barbie, the afternoon is the arvo, and g'day is a greeting. The outback is a remote area with very few people.
  5. Some Aboriginal words, such as kangaroo and boomerang, are known all over the world.
  6. In informal speech, yeah no can mean no, and no yeah can mean yes. The meaning is mostly in the last word.

Scottish English

  1. Scottish English is the English spoken in Scotland. Scots is a way of speaking in Scotland that is close to English. Scottish English grew out of contact between Scots and the English of England after the seventeenth century.
  2. Many Scots speakers use Scottish English in formal situations and Scots in informal ones. Some switch clearly from one to the other.
  3. Here Scottish English is different: in most places in Scotland, the r at the end of a syllable is pronounced.
  4. Typical words are wee for small, bairn for child, bonnie for pretty and kirk for church. The ending -ie makes things smaller: a young boy can be a laddie and a young girl a lassie.
  5. Some words are used in a different way. Where do you stay? means Where do you live?

Merkmale erkennen

Merke

Eine Varietät hat Merkmale in vier Bereichen: Aussprache, Wortschatz, Schreibung und Grammatik.

Beim Wortschatz teilt das australische Englisch manche Wörter mit dem britischen und andere mit dem amerikanischen: Für den Lastwagen sagt man in Australien truck wie im amerikanischen, für das Handy mobile phone wie im britischen Englisch.

Die Lesehilfe in der Wortliste ist nur eine Annäherung an das Lesen der Wörter. Sie ahmt keinen Akzent nach. Wie eine Varietät klingt, hörst du am besten von Menschen, die sie sprechen.
