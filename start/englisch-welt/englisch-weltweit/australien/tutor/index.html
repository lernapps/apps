Ein Land, ein Kontinent, viele Völker

The land

  1. Australia is a country and a continent at the same time. It consists of the mainland, the island of Tasmania and many smaller islands.
  2. It lies between the Indian Ocean and the Pacific Ocean. In the north, the Timor Sea separates it from Asia, and the Tasman Sea lies between Australia and New Zealand.
  3. It is the flattest and driest continent on which people live. About seventy per cent of the land is dry or semi-arid. By area, Australia is the sixth-largest country in the world.
  4. So most people live near the coast. About ninety-five per cent of the Australians live within a hundred kilometres of the sea.
  5. Canberra is the capital. Sydney and Melbourne are bigger: both have more than five million people.
  6. The Great Dividing Range runs along the east of the country. The Great Barrier Reef, the largest coral reef in the world, lies off the north-east coast and is more than two thousand three hundred kilometres long. Because Australia was cut off from other continents for a very long time, much of its wildlife is unique.

The first peoples and the colony

  1. People have lived in Australia for a very long time. Researchers think that the ancestors of the Aboriginal Australians arrived between fifty thousand and sixty-five thousand years ago.
  2. "Aboriginal Australians" does not mean one single people. When the British arrived, the Aboriginal people spoke more than two hundred and fifty different languages.
  3. The Torres Strait Islanders are a different people with their own culture and languages. Their islands lie at the northern tip of Australia.
  4. In 1770, James Cook sailed along the east coast and claimed it for Great Britain. In 1788, the First Fleet arrived at Sydney Cove with more than a thousand people, among them 778 convicts. Britain wanted a prison colony there.
  5. The British made their camp at Sydney Cove on 26 January 1788. This day later became Australia's national day, Australia Day. Some Aboriginal Australians call 26 January Invasion Day or Survival Day, or they mark it as a Day of Mourning.

Then and now

  1. For Aboriginal people, the settlers brought great losses. The British signed no treaties with them. Many people died of diseases that the settlers brought, others were killed in fights over land, and the settlers took most of the land.
  2. From 1886, colonial governments began to take many Aboriginal children away from their families. They gave child protection and assimilation as their reasons. In some places this went on until the 1970s.
  3. On 1 January 1901, the six colonies became one country. Canberra was built as its capital, and until 1927 Melbourne served as the temporary capital.
  4. In 1967, a referendum changed the constitution: Aboriginal Australians were now counted in the census, and the federal parliament could make laws for them. In 1992, the High Court decided in the Mabo case that native title had survived: Australia was not "land belonging to no one" when the British came.
  5. On 13 February 2008, Prime Minister Kevin Rudd apologised in the federal parliament for the policy of taking children away. In the census of 2021, 3.7 per cent of the people in Australia were Aboriginal, and almost 77,000 Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people spoke an Indigenous language at home.

Australien auf der Karte und in der Zeit

Bild: Schemakarte Australiens. Im Südosten liegt die Hauptstadt Canberra als Stern, östlich davon an der Küste Sydney und weiter im Süden Melbourne. Entlang der Ostseite verläuft die Great Dividing Range, vor der Nordostküste als gestrichelte Linie das Great Barrier Reef. Südlich des Festlands liegt die Insel Tasmania. Bildunterschrift: Schemakarte, nicht genau nach Maßstab. Norden ist oben. Der Stern ist die Hauptstadt, die Punkte sind die beiden größten Städte.

  • 1770: James Cook an der Ostküste – Cook zeichnet die Küste und beansprucht sie für Großbritannien.
  • 1788-01-26 (26. Januar 1788): First Fleet in Sydney Cove – Die Briten gründen eine Strafkolonie. Der Tag wird später Nationalfeiertag.
  • 1901-01-01 (1. Januar 1901): Die sechs Kolonien werden ein Staat – Australien ist nun ein Staat innerhalb des britischen Weltreichs.
  • 1911: Das Gebiet der Hauptstadt entsteht – Das Gebiet für die künftige Hauptstadt Canberra wird eingerichtet.
  • 1967: Volksabstimmung über die Verfassung – Aboriginal Australians werden bei der Volkszählung mitgezählt.
  • 1992: Mabo-Entscheidung – Das oberste Gericht erkennt das Recht der ersten Völker auf ihr Land an.
