Three boxes: input, process, output

Jona types 3 + 4 =. A moment later the display shows 7. In between, the calculator works in three steps. In the picture each step is a box with its own colour and letter: I blue, P green, O orange. The arrows show the order in which the data travels.

PProcessprocessor works out 7, memory holds the numbers
⇅storage – keepsstorage, a storage chip
OOutputdisplay: 7

The IPO picture: three boxes with arrows in this order. Box I “Input” (blue): the keys, 3 + 4 =. Box P “Process” (green): the processor works out 7, memory holds the numbers. Box O “Output” (orange): the display shows 7. A dashed drawer is attached to the box Process: “storage – keeps”, a storage chip. A double arrow shows that data goes into it and comes back out.

IPO model

This is how every computer works: Input (data goes in), Process (the processor works with the data in memory), Output (the result comes out).

The calculator forgets the 7 straight away. A tablet wants to keep many things, for example a photo. For that the box Process has a drawer: storage. In the picture it says “storage – keeps”. That is why some books call it the IPOS model, with S for storage.

Remember

Input means: into the computer. Output means: out of the computer, to you or to another part, for example to the drum of a washing machine. A loudspeaker brings sounds out of the computer. That is why it is an output device.

Join in: we are a computer

A game for three or four children, without a computer

  1. Share out the roles input, process and output. Each role gets a sign.
  2. Input thinks of two small numbers, writes them on a note and passes it on.
  3. Process adds the numbers and writes only the result on a new note.
  4. Output calls out the result to the class.
  5. A fourth child can be storage: process hands them a copy of the result. They keep it in a box and give it back to process when asked.
  6. Swap roles. From input to output the notes only travel one way, like the arrows in the IPO picture.

Exercise

Three worked tasks, each with its solution:

  1. Task no. 1

    Jona works out 3 + 4 on the calculator. Put the three steps in the right order.

    Hint

    For each step, ask: does something go in, is something worked on, or does something come out?

    Solution
    1. Input, because something goes in here: Jona presses 3, +, 4 and = on the keys.
    2. Process, because this is where the work is done: The processor works out 3 + 4 = 7.
    3. Output, because the result comes out here: The display shows 7.

    Right answer: 1. Jona presses 3, +, 4 and = on the keys, 2. The processor works out 3 + 4 = 7, 3. The display shows 7

  2. Task no. 2

    Jona listens to a voice message. Put the three steps in the right order.

    Hint

    For each step, ask: does something go in, is something worked on, or does something come out?

    Solution
    1. Input, because something goes in here: Jona taps “Play”.
    2. Process, because this is where the work is done: The processor turns the message into sounds.
    3. Output, because the result comes out here: The loudspeaker plays the message.

    Right answer: 1. Jona taps “Play”, 2. The processor turns the message into sounds, 3. The loudspeaker plays the message

  3. Task no. 3

    Selin takes a photo with the tablet. Put the three steps in the right order.

    Hint

    For each step, ask: does something go in, is something worked on, or does something come out?

    Solution
    1. Input, because something goes in here: The camera takes the picture.
    2. Process, because this is where the work is done: The processor makes the picture brighter and sharper.
    3. Output, because the result comes out here: The screen shows the finished photo.

    Right answer: 1. The camera takes the picture, 2. The processor makes the picture brighter and sharper, 3. The screen shows the finished photo
