Station 6: Mia is looking for a device

At station 5 you put devices and parts into the IPO boxes. Now Mia, 11, from class 6b comes to visit. She has to write a report about hedgehogs, two pages of text. She asks Jona: “Which device should I use?”

On this page you learn to choose a suitable device and program for a task and to explain your choice.

Choosing with the IPO picture

Every device has different input devices and output devices. The table compares the three devices on the table.

What the three devices have for input and output
DeviceInput (I) and output (O)
smartwatchI: tiny touchscreen, motion detector. O: tiny screen.
smartphoneI: touchscreen, camera, microphone, motion detector. O: small screen, loudspeaker.
laptopI: keyboard, touchpad, camera, microphone. O: big screen, loudspeaker.

Jona shows Mia the IPO picture for her report. This way she sees what the task needs:

IInputtype a lot of text: keyboard
PProcessprogram: word processor
⇅storage – keepsthe report
OOutputsee a whole page: big screen

The IPO picture for Mia's report: box I “Input”: type a lot of text, so a keyboard. Arrow to box P “Process”: the program word processor. Arrow to box O “Output”: see a whole page, so a big screen. The drawer “storage – keeps” with the report hangs on Process.

word processor

A program for writing and laying out texts.

presentation program

A program for slides that you show during a talk.

How to choose: three questions

Mia's report

  1. What should come out? Two pages of text that Mia can read well. Output: a big screen.
  2. What do I have to put in? Lots of words. Input: a keyboard.
  3. Which device and program can do that? The laptop with a word processor.

Mia's reason: “I'll take the laptop, because I have to type a lot and want to see the whole page.”

Remember

The newest or most expensive device is not always the best fit. It depends on the task.

Exercise

Three worked tasks, each with its solution:

  1. Task no. 1

    Jona wants to take photos on the class hike and look at them straight away. Which device fits best?

    • Smartwatch
    • Smartphone
    • Laptop
    Hint

    Go through the three questions: what should come out? What do I have to put in? Which device can do that?

    Solution
    1. Photos need a camera for input and a screen for output, and the device has to fit in a pocket. The smartphone can do that.

    Right answer: Smartphone

  2. Task no. 2

    Mia wants to see the time on her bike without stopping. Which device fits best?

    • Smartwatch
    • Smartphone
    • Laptop
    Hint

    Go through the three questions: what should come out? What do I have to put in? Which device can do that?

    Solution
    1. The smartwatch shows the time on the wrist. A quick look is enough.

    Right answer: Smartwatch

  3. Task no. 3

    Selin films a short video of the dance at the school fair. Which device fits best?

    • Smartwatch
    • Smartphone
    • Laptop
    Hint

    Go through the three questions: what should come out? What do I have to put in? Which device can do that?

    Solution
    1. A video needs a camera and a microphone for input. The smartphone has both and is easy to hold.

    Right answer: Smartphone
