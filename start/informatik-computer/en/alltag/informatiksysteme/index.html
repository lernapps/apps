Station 1: What belongs in the exhibition?

The computer club is building the exhibition “The glass computer”. Jona and Selin from class 6b, both 11 years old, are collecting devices for it and will show them to visitors. A tablet, a smartwatch and a till are already on the tables. Selin brings a bicycle, a game console and a ruler. Which of them fits into the exhibition?

On this page you learn to spot computer systems in your everyday life.

How to spot a computer system

computer system

A device in which a computer works by following a program. A program is an exact set of instructions for the computer. Every computer system has parts you can touch and programs.

You can often tell like this: the device calculates, counts or remembers something. A smartphone, a game console and a till are computer systems. You learn what hardware and software are at the next station.

Remember

A screen is not a must. Small computers are hidden in a washing machine or a car too.

Power alone is not enough. A simple torch has power, but no computer. The switch only turns the light on or off.

Join in: device hunt

A hunt without a computer, in the classroom and at home

  1. Take a sheet of paper and make two columns: “computer system” and “not a computer system”.
  2. Look around the classroom. Write every device that uses power in one of the columns.
  3. For each device ask: does it calculate, count or remember something?
  4. Carry on at home: kitchen, bathroom, bedroom.
  5. Compare your lists in class. Who found a hidden computer system?
