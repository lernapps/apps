How you start

  • “Be my tutor”: first ask what the child already knows about the topic. Then go through the page in order, one section per message: first why the topic matters, then the rule, then the example. After each section check with a short question on its content that the child understood it; a “yes” is not enough. Only once it can explain the example, send it to the app with the link to its task.
  • “Help me with exercise …, no. …” (the child comes from “Help” in the app): look the exercise and the number up below and start right at this task. If it says “The app says: …”, find the mistake (F1, F2 …) of the exercise that the message fits: that is the child's mistake. If the message names an input and it is in a column F… for this number, that confirms the mistake. Say in one sentence what about exactly this input does not fit the task, and do not repeat the message. If both are missing, ask what the child entered or built and where it is stuck. Then give a hint or the next step in words, and send it back to the app to go on.

Special requirements

If the address of this page names bedarf=, each section it names holds, several separated by commas. It goes before what is said above; if two contradict each other, the stricter one holds. Without bedarf= none holds.

  • kurze-schritte: Unlike above: two short sentences at most, one thought per message, each part of a section with a question of its own. Say how far there is still to go.

Context: the page “Using programs”

Open, save, copy, paste and cut in a drawing program.

Station 3: the poster for the exhibition

You now know hardware and software. Now you use a piece of software yourself. Yesterday Selin drew a poster for the exhibition on the computer. Then she closed the drawing program. Today the poster is gone.

On this page you learn the most important commands of a program: open, save, copy, paste and cut.

  • Exercise plakat-weg (below)

The most important commands

Picture: A drawing program. At the top there are six buttons: a folder, a floppy disk, scissors, two sheets, a clipboard and an arrow pointing back. Below is a drawing area with a yellow sun. A dashed frame marks the sun. Caption: An invented drawing program. The numbers belong to the commands below. The dashed frame shows: the sun is selected. Labels: 1 open, 2 save, 3 cut, 4 copy, 5 paste, 6 undo

What each command does:

  • Command | What happens

  • Open | brings a saved picture back onto the screen

  • Save | keeps the picture, even after closing

  • Copy | puts a copy of what is selected on the clipboard

  • Paste | puts in what is on the clipboard

  • Cut | takes away what is selected and puts it on the clipboard

  • Undo | takes back the last step

  • clipboard: An invisible place where the computer keeps what you copied or cut until you paste it. That is how you can also paste from one program into another.

Remember: Remember

Copying does not put anything in yet. Only pasting brings the copy into the picture. You can paste it several times. Cut takes the original away, copy does not.

You usually find open and save in the “File” menu. On a tablet there are often no keys for copy and paste. Then you find the commands in a menu, for example when you press and hold something. On a keyboard it works like this, for example: copy with Ctrl + C, paste with Ctrl + V, cut with Ctrl + X, undo with Ctrl + Z. On a Mac you use ⌘ instead of Ctrl.

Example: two suns for the poster

Example: Selin needs a second sun

  1. Selin selects the sun. A dashed frame appears.
  2. She chooses copy. There is still one sun in the picture. The copy is on the clipboard.
  3. She chooses paste. Now there are two suns. She moves the new one to its place.
  4. She chooses save and names the picture “Poster”. Now she can close the program.

Remember: Join in: in a real drawing program

Open a drawing program on a school computer or tablet. Draw a sun, copy it and paste it twice. Save the picture under your first name, close the program and open the picture again. Are all three suns still there?

Exercise plakat-weg

  • Task: Why is Selin's poster gone?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ She did not save it before closing – What is not saved is lost when you close the program.
    • ✗ The drawing program deletes all pictures every night – A drawing program does not delete saved pictures by itself.
    • ✗ The screen was too small for the poster – The screen only shows what is open right now. It does not keep anything.
  • Solution: She did not save it before closing
  • Explanation: You have to save before closing. Otherwise your work is gone.

Exercise sonne-ins-plakat

  • Task: Selin wants to bring the sun from an old picture into the poster. Both pictures are already open. In which order does she do it?
  • Answer format: put the items in the right order (the app shows them shuffled)
  • Solution: 1. Select the sun in the old picture, 2. Choose copy, 3. Switch to the poster, 4. Choose paste, 5. Save the poster
  • Explanation: First select, then copy. You can only paste once you are in the poster. Saving comes last so nothing gets lost.
  • Link: ../?via=tutor#sonne-ins-plakat

Exercise befehl-wirkung

  • Task: What happens with which command?
  • Answer format: match each item on the left with one on the right
  • Solution: copy – A copy goes onto the clipboard.; paste – What is on the clipboard goes into the picture.; cut – What is selected disappears and goes onto the clipboard.; open – A saved picture comes back onto the screen.; undo – The last step is taken back.; save – The picture is kept even after closing.
  • Explanation: Printing is a command of its own. It belongs to none of the six commands here.
  • Link: ../?via=tutor#befehl-wirkung

Exercise schliessen-speichert

  • Task: True or false? When you close a program, it always saves your work by itself.
  • Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
  • Solution: The statement is false.
  • Explanation: False. Many programs do ask, but not all of them. Save yourself before you close.
