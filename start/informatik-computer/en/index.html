How a computer works

The computer club is showing the exhibition “The glass computer”. Jona and Selin from class 6b, both 11, show you the stations. Later their classmate Mia comes to visit. You find computer systems in your everyday life, look inside a computer and learn the IPO model: input, process, output.

Contents

  1. Computer systems everywhere

    You find computer systems in your everyday life, tell hardware from software and use a drawing program.

    1. Computer systems in everyday life Spot computer systems: which devices have a small computer inside?
    2. Hardware and software Tell hardware from software and explain how they work together.
    3. Using programs Open, save, copy, paste and cut in a drawing program.
  2. A look inside: the IPO model

    You look inside the glass computer, put its parts into the three IPO boxes and choose a device for a task.

    1. Parts of a computer The glass computer: processor, memory, storage, input and output devices and their jobs.
    2. The IPO model Input, process, output: sort devices and steps into the three IPO boxes.
    3. Choosing devices and programs Choose a suitable device and program for a task and explain your choice with the IPO picture.

Media overview

Learn with an AI tutor

An AI tutor is an AI assistant of your choice that accompanies you while you learn with this app. It explains the topic with the examples of the pages, gives you tasks from the app and helps you when you are stuck. You solve the tasks in the app: it checks your answers.

Choose your assistant here. It is then ready under the title of every page, and every exercise has the button “Help”. You can also switch it on under the title of a page.

You use your own account with the provider. The assistant is not offered by lernapps.net: lernapps only provides the content and the exercises.

Your choice stays on this device; only your click opens the assistant. Ask your parents or teacher whether you may use it.
