Typonaut

Learn touch typing on a journey through space. Words fly across the screen in an arc; type them correctly and they turn into stardust. Chapter by chapter new keys are added, from the home row to the whole keyboard, with a German or an English keyboard.

Built by Oliver

Open the app ↗ Opens in a new tab. This page stays open.

May you use it?

  • No sign-up: nobody needs an account.
  • No installation: the app runs in the browser, on any device.
  • Free and without ads.
  • What people enter stays on the device. At most, the app remembers progress in the browser.
  • No connection to third-party servers.

Not usable with a screen reader and not meant for phones: the app needs a real keyboard, and the words fly towards the spaceship on their own.

The app was checked: when it was listed, an automatic check compared these statements with the app.

How the app gets to your class

To scan with tablets or phones

  • On the projector: open the app and switch to full screen.
  • On the class's devices: share the link or show the QR code.
  • At home: send your child the link. No account needed.

Straight to a topic

Each link leads to exactly this topic in the app.

  • German with a German keyboard (QWERTZ)
  • English with an English keyboard (QWERTY)
