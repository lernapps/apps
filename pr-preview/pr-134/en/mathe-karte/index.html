Mathematik · Grade 8

Mathe-Karte

Mathe selbst ausprobieren und üben: Prozentrechnung, binomische Formeln und Wahrscheinlichkeit. Zu jedem Thema gibt es eine Erklärung mit Bildern und Videos und beliebig viele Aufgaben mit sofortiger Rückmeldung.

Built by Ralf D. Müller

Open the app ↗ Opens in a new tab. This page stays open.

May you use it?

  • No sign-up: nobody needs an account.
  • No installation: the app runs in the browser, on any device.
  • Free and without ads.
  • What people enter stays on the device. At most, the app remembers progress in the browser.
  • Third-party servers only after a click. Erst wenn jemand ein Erklärvideo startet, lädt es von YouTube.

The app was checked: when it was listed, an automatic check compared these statements with the app.

How the app gets to your class

To scan with tablets or phones

  • On the projector: open the app and switch to full screen.
  • On the class's devices: share the link or show the QR code.
  • At home: send your child the link. No account needed.
