4 apps

  • Geography · Grades 5 to 10

    Naturwunder-Quiz

    In the quiz, students answer questions about natural wonders of the Earth: mountains and volcanoes, waterfalls, deserts, caves, coasts and reefs on all continents. After each answer, they read a short note on every option and can read on in the Wikipedia article.

    • ✓ No sign-up
    • ✓ No installation
    • ✓ Free

    Matches your search:

    • Mountains and volcanoes
    • Waterfalls, rivers and lakes
    • Deserts
    • Caves and gorges
    • Coasts, islands and reefs
    • Natural wonders by continent

  • Computer Science · Grades 3 to 13

    Typonaut

    Learn touch typing on a journey through space. Words fly across the screen in an arc; type them correctly and they turn into stardust. Chapter by chapter new keys are added, from the home row to the whole keyboard, with a German or an English keyboard.

    • ✓ No sign-up
    • ✓ No installation
    • ✓ Free

    Matches your search:

    • German with a German keyboard (QWERTZ)
    • English with an English keyboard (QWERTY)

  • Physics · Grades 5 to 8

    Electricity

    Learners work out step by step how a circuit works, which materials conduct electricity and how series and parallel circuits differ. In grades 7 and 8, current, voltage, resistance and Ohm's law follow. On every page they practise with a circuit in which you can see the current flow, build circuits and measure. The app is available in German and English.

    • ✓ No sign-up
    • ✓ No installation
    • ✓ Free

    • Series circuit
    • Parallel circuit

    • Voltage
    • Resistance
    • Ohm's law
    • Series circuit with meters
    • Parallel circuit with meters
    • Effects of electric current
    • Handling electricity safely
