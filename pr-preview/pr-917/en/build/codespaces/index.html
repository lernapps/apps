Build and practise with Codespaces

You do not have to install anything on your computer. A codespace is a ready-made workplace in the browser. Your AI assistant is already in it.

Rather build with an assistant on your own computer?

How it works

You need:

  • A free GitHub account.
  • A subscription or a key for an AI assistant: Claude (Claude Pro or higher) or ChatGPT (with Codex).
  • You pay for the AI yourself. lernapps.net does not pay for AI use.

You can do two things here: build an app and share it, or practise for an occasion, such as a test. The steps are the same, except in three places. They are marked “Build” and “Practise”.

  1. Open the template on GitHub (github.com/lernapps/start). Click “Use this template”, then “Create a new repository”. Give it a short name, for example “fractions”. The repository belongs to you.
    • Build: This repository becomes the repository of your app and stays.
    • Practise: This repository is only your workplace. It can go afterwards.
  2. Open your new repository. Click “Code”, then “Codespaces” and “Create codespace on main”. GitHub starts the workplace. This takes about two minutes.
  3. The editor asks whether you trust the authors of the files in this folder. Click “Trust folder and continue”. Without this yes the extensions for Claude and Codex stay off.
  4. Sign in to your assistant. How is described below for Claude and for ChatGPT. Signing in to Claude, Signing in to ChatGPT (Codex)
  5. Tell the assistant what you want. It puts everything in this folder, not in a subfolder. You confirm its plan and the interim results.
    • Build: Which app you want to build. It asks for the topic, the grade and what learners do.
    • Practise: What you want to practise for, for example “for the maths test next week”. Give it photos of the worksheets, with the subject, the grade and what is covered.
  6. At the end the assistant checks the app.
    • Build: It helps you publish it. How to publish your app
    • Practise: It gives you the ZIP file with the folder “src”. Submit it in the form and get the link to the app. Submitting step by step

What your workplace looks like

Once the codespace is open you see a window like this. The red speech bubbles explain the main parts.

  • Left: The files of your app. They are created bit by bit.
  • Top right of the editor: Here you start your assistant: Claude or Codex (ChatGPT).
  • Right: The sign-in opens here. Sometimes it causes problems. How to solve them is further down.
  • Middle: The guide (README). Read it, at least the first time.
  • Bottom: The terminal. You probably do not need it and can minimise it.

The screenshots are in German, like your codespace if your browser is set to German. Click a picture to see it larger.

Signing in to Claude

Click the icon of Claude at the top right of the editor. A window opens on the right. Choose “Claude.ai Subscription”, then “Continue in browser”.

The first time, signing in probably fails. The browser then says that the page “localhost” cannot be reached. That is normal in a codespace.

Copy the address from the window of Claude (button next to the field) and paste it again into a new tab of your browser. Then signing in continues. If the page shows a code, copy it and paste it into the window of Claude. The editor sometimes asks whether it may open the web page: click “Open”.

Signing in to ChatGPT (Codex)

Click the icon of Codex at the top right of the editor. Do not choose “Sign in with ChatGPT” but “More options” and then signing in with a device code (“device flow”). It shows you a code that you enter on the page of ChatGPT.

You have to allow the device code in ChatGPT once, in the settings under security: “Enable device code login for Codex …”. Never share a device code with anyone.

Codex asks what it may do on its own. You work in a separate environment: even with “Full access” your computer is not affected. Your account and everything you type in the codespace are. Do not type passwords there.

Pausing and continuing later

By default a codespace stops by itself after 30 minutes without input (auto-pause). A codespace you do not open for 30 days is deleted. You can change both at GitHub.

  • When it stops, your files stay, even changes that are not saved yet. Running work of the assistant and the preview end.
  • To continue, open your codespaces on GitHub (button below) and click “Open” at your codespace. That takes about a minute. Or open “Code” in the repository, then “Codespaces”, and choose the existing one. Do not create a new one: it would be empty.
  • Because everything is deleted after 30 days without use: ask the assistant at the end of a session to save your work on GitHub.
  • A stopped codespace costs no computing time, only a little storage. You set the waiting time and the retention at GitHub under “Settings” and “Codespaces”.

Good to know

  • GitHub gives free accounts about 60 hours of codespace time each month. Stop the codespace when you are done.
  • The assistant may do anything in your codespace and sees the whole internet. Do not type passwords or data of pupils there.
  • Your texts go to the provider of the assistant (Anthropic or OpenAI). Do not write names of learners in them.
  • The finished app collects no data and runs in the browser without an account. lernapps.net checks that before it appears. When you practise, content and app are public, so without names and school.
