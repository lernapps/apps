Schreibwerkstatt: Sachtexte nach Bauplan

Du schreibst englische Sachtexte nach einem Bauplan: Bericht, Interview, Blog und Bewerbung. Du stellst den Ton zwischen informell und formell ein und liest Zeitungstexte und Statistiken. Du brauchst Ton.

Built by Oliver (@mrsimpson)

Start ↗ Opens in a new tab. This page stays open.

May you use it?

  • No sign-up: nobody needs an account.
  • No installation: the app runs in the browser, on any device.
  • Free and without ads.
  • What people enter stays on the device. At most, the app remembers progress in the browser.
  • No connection to third-party servers.

The app was checked: when it was listed, an automatic check compared these statements with the app.

How the app gets to your class

To scan with tablets or phones

  • On the projector: open the app and switch to full screen.
  • On the class's devices: share the link or show the QR code.
  • At home: send your child the link. No account needed.

On the learning map

This app practises building blocks of several strands of English: Vocabulary, Countries and cultures, Texts and media.

It practises these building blocks:

  • Jobs and careers
  • Formal and informal language
  • British and American school systems
  • Structuring a text
  • Report and article
  • Interview
  • Blog post
  • Fiction and non-fiction
  • Presentation
  • Describing charts
  • Formal email and letter
  • CV
  • Letter of application
  • Job interview
  • Structure of a newspaper article
  • Fact and opinion
  • Changing the text type
