How to read the map

Strand · a column
A part of the subject that runs over several school years.
Building block · a box
One thing a child can learn: “I can …”.
Grade band · a row
Roughly when a building block is taught. Choose a curriculum to see its grades.
App · the colour
Blue means covered: an app practises the building block. Dashed means a gap: no app practises it yet.

You see the curriculum Hesse, Grammar school (G9). It places 123 of 128 building blocks in a grade. A building block stands in the first grade in which it is taught. “Again in grade …” says when the curriculum takes it up again. Building blocks without a grade stay in the striped row of their grade band.

Not captured yet: Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, Berlin, Brandenburg, Bremen, Hamburg, Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, Lower Saxony, North Rhine-Westphalia, Rhineland-Palatinate, Saarland, Saxony, Saxony-Anhalt, Schleswig-Holstein, Thuringia
