Geography · Grades 5 to 10

Naturwunder-Quiz

In the quiz, students answer questions about natural wonders of the Earth: mountains and volcanoes, waterfalls, deserts, caves, coasts and reefs on all continents. After each answer, they read a short note on every option and can read on in the Wikipedia article.

Built by Oliver

May you use it?

  • No sign-up: nobody needs an account.
  • No installation: the app runs in the browser, on any device.
  • Free and without ads.
  • The app stores nothing.
  • No connection to third-party servers.

These are the app's own statements. We are building an automatic check.

How the app gets to your class

To scan with tablets or phones

  • On the projector: open the app and switch to full screen.
  • On the class's devices: share the link or show the QR code.
  • At home: send your child the link. No account needed.
