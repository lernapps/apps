# Licence of the basic vocabulary of French

The files of this folder (`source.yaml`, `words.yaml`) are derived from **FLELex / Beacco** (CEFRLex, CENTAL,
UCLouvain, <https://cental.uclouvain.be/cefrlex/flelex/download/>) and are licensed, as FLELex is, under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
The MIT licence of this repository does not apply to them.

Changes to FLELex: only the words of the levels A1 and A2, without the frequencies; a few levels corrected, each
with its reason in `source.yaml` and in the `note` of the word.

Please cite FLELex as its authors ask:

- François, T., Gala, N., Watrin, P. & Fairon, C. (2014). FLELex: a graded lexical resource for French foreign
  learners. In Proceedings of the 9th International Conference on Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC 2014),
  Reykjavik, Iceland.
- Pintard, A. & François, T. (2020). Combining expert knowledge with frequency information to infer CEFR levels for
  words. In Proceedings of the 1st Workshop on Tools and Resources to Empower People with REAding DIfficulties
  (READI), pp. 85-92.
